Definitions

Learners reached
Learners who used TABASAM for learning or assessment during the month.
Marked as improved
Learners recorded as "Improved" rather than "Needs Support" in the month, divided by learners reached.
Learning institutions
Schools, centres and other institutions with learners or educators using TABASAM in the month.
Educators
Teachers and other educators using TABASAM in the month.
All months
The opening view. Learner figures add up the monthly reports, so a learner reached in several months is counted in each. Institutions and educators are the highest figure in any one month.
Change
Difference from the previous month shown in the dashboard; for improved share, in percentage points.
