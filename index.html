How TABASAM works

TABASAM is TUSOME's AI-powered screening tool for Tanzanian classrooms: identify learning needs early and support every learner.

  1. AssessTeachers screen each learner in literacy, numeracy, social and emotional learning and behaviour, online or offline.
  2. AnalyseTABASAM's AI reviews the results to identify patterns and learners who need additional support.
  3. Generate a learning planIt creates an Individual Education Plan (IEP) for each learner, with practical recommendations for the teacher.
  4. Support and trackTeachers act on the plan, with training and mentoring from TUSOME, and track progress as learners are reassessed.

Learn more about TABASAM at screen.tusome.ac.tz

    Definitions

    Learners reached
    Learners who used TABASAM for learning or assessment during the month.
    Learners demonstrating improved learning outcomes
    Learners recorded as "Improved" rather than "Needs Support", as a share of learners reached in months when outcomes were assessed (from January 2026).
    Learning institutions
    Schools, centres and other institutions with learners or educators using TABASAM in the month.
    Learners assessed
    Learners with a TABASAM outcome recorded in the month ("Improved" or "Needs Support").
    Identified for support
    Learners recorded as "Needs Support" in the month: TABASAM's diagnostic result that a learner needs additional help.
    Educators reached
    Teachers and other educators using TABASAM in the month.
    MCF EdTech Fellowship target
    Beneficiaries (learners plus educators) added up from the first monthly report to the month shown, against the fellowship target.
    All months
    The opening view: totals across all monthly reports. Each learner is counted once.
    Change
    Difference from the previous month shown in the dashboard; for improved share, in percentage points.
