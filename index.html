Definitions

Learners reached
Learners who used TABASAM for learning or assessment during the month.
Marked as improved
Learners recorded as "Improved" rather than "Needs Support", divided by learners reached in months when outcomes were assessed. Learning outcomes have been assessed since January 2026.
Learning institutions
Schools, centres and other institutions with learners or educators using TABASAM in the month.
Identified for support
Learners recorded as "Needs Support" in the month: TABASAM's diagnostic result that a learner needs additional help.
Educators
Teachers and other educators using TABASAM in the month.
MCF EdTech Fellowship target
Beneficiaries (learners plus educators) added up from the first monthly report to the month shown, against the fellowship target.
All months
The opening view: totals across all monthly reports. Each learner is counted once.
Change
Difference from the previous month shown in the dashboard; for improved share, in percentage points.
