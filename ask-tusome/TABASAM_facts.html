  • 2026-07 · Product: Development focused on connecting learner assessment results with practical recommendations and professional development guidance for teachers.
  • 2026-07 · Implementation: TABASAM implementation continued through follow-up with participating schools, institutions and communities.
  • 2026-06 · Implementation: Learners took part in TABASAM-supported learning activities during a June school-holiday programme.

  • 2026-06 · Product: TUSOME and HWPL Korea began testing how peace education competencies could fit within TABASAM's social and emotional learning assessments.

How the figures are counted (read before quoting)

  • Learners, gender, level and region come from each report's learner list, not the summary sheet. Where a summary sheet reports more learners than its list (June 2026; January, March, April and May 2026), learners reached uses the summary figure, so breakdowns add up to slightly less than the total.
  • Each month's learners are treated as different learners (TUSOME's instruction); all-months totals add the months up. Institutions and educators are also added up, so the same school or teacher can be counted once per month they appear.
  • Learning outcomes were not measured March–December 2025 (all learners “Too early to measure”); improved share uses only months with outcomes.
  • November 2025 counts all 2,116 list records, including 150 dated October 2025.
  • Entered by hand on TUSOME's instruction: July 2026 institutions (8); December 2025 one learner without gender counted as female; May 2026 ten educators without gender split equally.
  • Learners with no region are counted in Dar es Salaam; semi-urban is counted as rural.
  • March 2025 (entered by hand, TUSOME's instruction): 532 beneficiaries reached before the first monthly report, recorded as 343 learners and 189 educators, 53% female (182 girls, 161 boys; 100 female, 89 male educators). No region, level, disability, institution or outcome breakdown.
  • Public figures elsewhere may differ: screen.tusome.ac.tz shows 8,673 learners, 670 teachers and 75 partner schools (period not stated). Use this document's figures for MCF/Sahara reporting and say which period they cover.
